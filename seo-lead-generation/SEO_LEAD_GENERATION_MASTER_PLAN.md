## Weekly SEO Routine
- Publish 1-2 blogs.
- Share every blog on LinkedIn.
- Check Search Console queries and indexed pages.
- Request indexing for new URLs.
- Review form submissions and source data.
- Improve one existing page based on impressions/clicks.

## Monthly SEO Routine
- Add 2-4 quality backlinks/mentions.
- Build one new landing page.
- Refresh one old blog.
- Add one case-study style article.
- Review PageSpeed mobile and desktop.
- Review conversion rates by page.

## Lead Follow-up Workflow
0-15 minutes: reply by email/WhatsApp.
Day 1: send discovery call link.
Day 2: send relevant audit/checklist insight.
Day 4: ask one pain-point question.
Day 7: send one case-style article or ChargePilot info.
Day 14: final soft follow-up.
Monthly: add to newsletter.
