# Recommended Information Architecture

## Navigation principle

Organize the website by the visitor’s task and buying intent, not by Resolute MSO’s internal department list. Keep the primary navigation to six decision paths and move long-tail SEO pages into clearly linked hubs.

## Primary navigation

1. **RCM Services**
   - End-to-End Revenue Cycle Management
   - Medical Billing and Coding
   - Eligibility and Benefits Verification
   - Charge Entry and Claim Submission
   - Payment Posting and Reconciliation
   - AR Follow-Up and Recovery
   - Denial and Appeal Management
   - Credentialing and Provider Enrollment
   - Patient Billing Support
   - Reporting and Analytics

2. **Healthcare Automation**
   - Automation Overview
   - ChargePilot™
   - Claim Entry Automation
   - AR Workflow Automation
   - Eligibility Automation
   - Payment Posting Automation
   - Denial Workflow Automation
   - Reporting Automation
   - Custom RPA and AI Solutions

3. **Healthcare Technology**
   - Custom Healthcare Software
   - Executive RCM Dashboards
   - Practice Health Dashboard
   - Healthcare SaaS Development
   - System Integrations
   - API and Data Workflow Development
   - Website and Portal Development

4. **Staffing and Operations**
   - Remote Medical Billing Staff
   - Virtual Assistants
   - Administrative Support
   - RCM Team Extension
   - Business Development Support
   - Operational Process Improvement

5. **Who We Serve**
   - Physicians and Independent Practices
   - Provider and Multi-Specialty Groups
   - Clinical Laboratories
   - Pathology, Toxicology, and Molecular Labs
   - Imaging and Radiology Centers
   - Urgent Care Organizations
   - Medical Billing and RCM Companies
   - MSOs and Healthcare Executives
   - Specialty directory

6. **Resources**
   - RCM Insights
   - Medical Billing Guides
   - Automation and AI Insights
   - KPI and Benchmark Guides
   - Case Studies
   - FAQs and Glossary

Utility links: About, Quality, Compliance, Privacy, Careers, Contact, Book a Demo.

## CTA strategy

| Visitor intent | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA | Tertiary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| General homepage | Book a Demo | Request a Free Audit | Talk on WhatsApp |
| RCM service | Request a Free Audit | Book a Demo | Related service |
| Automation/ChargePilot | Book an Automation Demo | Request Workflow Assessment | Talk on WhatsApp |
| Staffing | Discuss Staffing Needs | Request Capability Overview | Email |
| Resource article | Request a Free Audit | Read related guide | Subscribe only after consent workflow is ready |
| About/compliance | Contact Resolute MSO | View Services | Quality/Privacy page |

Every commercial page should provide one next step above the fold and one contextual next step after proof or process content.

## Internal-link strategy

- Every service page links upward to its hub, sideways to two complementary services, downward to one relevant specialty page, and to one educational resource.
- Every specialty page links to the RCM stages most relevant to that specialty and one automation opportunity.
- Every resource page links to a commercial page only where the topic naturally supports it.
- ChargePilot pages link to implementation, supported workflows, security, demo, and relevant automation guides.
- Breadcrumbs must match the visible hierarchy and BreadcrumbList schema.
- Consolidate overlapping keyword pages with canonicalization or permanent redirects rather than maintaining thin near-duplicates.

## Priority content gaps

1. Evidence-governed case studies with baseline, period, methodology, result, and client approval.
2. ChargePilot supported-workflow and security architecture pages.
3. Healthcare technology and custom-software hub.
4. Staffing and operations hub.
5. Executive dashboard examples with clearly labeled illustrative data.
6. Integration and API capabilities.
7. Implementation process and onboarding expectations.
8. Service-level quality, escalation, and reporting model.
9. Author/reviewer profiles and reviewed dates for educational content.
10. Careers and employer-branding page.

## Migration approach

Preserve the highest-value existing URLs initially. Map every old `.html` and duplicate slug to one preferred directory URL, test permanent redirects, update all internal links, submit the new sitemap, and monitor coverage before removing legacy pages.
